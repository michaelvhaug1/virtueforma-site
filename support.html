How Can We Help?

Our support team is available to assist you with:

  • Account issues - Login problems, password resets, email verification
  • Subscription questions - Billing inquiries, plan changes, family plan setup
  • Technical support - App not working correctly, sync issues, bug reports
  • Feature questions - How to use activities, streaks, badges, groups, or parishes
  • Data requests - Export your data, account deletion, privacy questions
  • Feedback and suggestions - Ideas for new features or improvements

Before You Contact Us

For the fastest resolution, please include:

  • The email address associated with your Forma account
  • A description of the issue you're experiencing
  • Your device type (iPhone, Android) and app version if reporting a technical issue
  • Screenshots if applicable

We typically respond within 1-2 business days. For urgent account security issues, please include "URGENT" in your subject line.
